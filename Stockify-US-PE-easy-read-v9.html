5 risks you must understand

Only invest money you won’t need for many years, and can afford to lose.

  • You could lose money

    Private companies can fail, or be sold for less than you paid.

  • Your money is locked in

    There’s no easy way to sell before an IPO or sale. It may never list.

  • You own units, not shares

    A holding company keeps the shares for you.

  • Less information

    Private companies share far less than listed ones.

  • Currency and tax

    You invest in dollars, so currency moves change your return.

What you actually ownHow your money reaches the company’s shares

The “SPV” is a small US company set up for one deal only. It pools everyone’s money, buys the shares and gives you units.

Your money goes: your bank → the deal’s escrow account → shares, when the deal closes. If the company blocks the sale, escrow returns it.

Getting your money outIPO, lock-up and sale
  • The company lists or is sold

    There is no fixed date. It can take years.

  • Lock-up, usually 180 days

    After an IPO, shares usually can’t be sold for about 6 months.

  • You choose

    We sell and pay you, or move the shares to your own broker account.
