Frequently asked questions

Is my money safe in this MLD?

Your ₹1,00,000 is protected at maturity only if Kaveri can pay. Kaveri is rated CRISIL PP-MLD AA+ / Stable. If it defaults, you may lose part or all.

What happens if the Nifty falls 25%?

The final level is below the 80% barrier, so the condition is not met. You get ₹1,00,000 back on 12 Nov 2028, with no return.

Can I get more than 22.5% if the Nifty rallies?

No. The payout is ₹1,22,500 per debenture whether the Nifty is flat, up 10% or up 60%.

How is the final Nifty level fixed?

It is the average of the Nifty 50 closes on 23, 24 and 25 Oct 2028, compared with the close on 12 Oct 2026.

How much tax will I pay?

Gains are taxed at your income-tax slab, however long you hold. At the 30% slab plus cess, 10.23% a year becomes 7.15%.

How MLDs work

Market Linked Debentures, in plain English

An MLD is a debenture – a loan you give to an NBFC – that is listed on BSE. You get your money back at maturity if the issuer pays. On top, you may earn a return that depends on the Nifty or a government bond. This page shows how, what it costs you in tax, and what can go wrong.

Where your ₹1,00,000 goes

Most of it goes into the issuer’s lending, planned to grow back to ₹1,00,000. The small rest buys the chance of an extra return.
Illustration · the issuer sets the real split
Today You buy one or more debentures of ₹1,00,000 each, from a listed NBFC.
During the tenure The issuer puts most of the money to work in its loan book and bonds, and uses a small part to buy Nifty options.
At maturity You get ₹1,00,000 back if the issuer pays, plus the extra return only if the condition is met.

The 3 payoff types

Each chart shows your total return at maturity for where the index ends. Red = no return, just your ₹1,00,000 back.
MLD payout Bank FD 7.1%, same tenure, before tax

Conditional coupon

e.g. Kaveri Nifty 50 Booster – Series 7 · 25 months

A fixed-size payout if the index ends above a barrier. Below it, just your ₹1,00,000.

Good for: a view that the Nifty won’t fall more than 20% – you earn even if it falls a bit. Watch out: fall past the barrier and you earn 0%. Since 2000, the Nifty fell over 20% in 9% of 25-month windows (sample).

Participation with cap

e.g. Kaveri Nifty Participation – Series 8 · 36 months

You get a share of the index’s rise, up to a cap. If the index falls, just your ₹1,00,000.

Good for: a view that the market will rise steadily over 3 years. Watch out: a flat or falling Nifty gives 0%, and gains stop at 45% however far it rises.

Near-fixed (G-Sec)

e.g. Palash G-Sec Linked – Series 3 · 18 months

Linked to a government bond’s price, with a very low bar. Works almost like a fixed-rate debenture.

Good for: a steady, FD-like return from a rated NBFC, over a short tenure. Watch out: it is still a loan to an NBFC – the issuer risk is the real risk here.

Read a term sheet in 2 minutes

Eight words that decide what you get. Examples from Kaveri Series 7 and 8.
See them on a real product page
1Initial level
The index level on the start date. Everything is measured against it. Series 7: Nifty close on 12 Oct 2026
2Final level & averaging
The index level at the end – often an average of a few days, so one bad day matters less. Series 7: average of closes on 23, 24 and 25 Oct 2028
3Barrier
The line the final level must stay at or above for you to get the coupon. Series 7: 80% of initial · start 26,000 → barrier 20,800
4Participation
How much of the index’s rise you get. Series 8: 120% · Nifty +10% gives you +12%
5Cap
The most you can earn, however far the index rises. Series 8: 45% total, reached at Nifty +37.5%
6PP-MLD rating
A credit rating for principal-protected MLDs. It rates the issuer’s ability to pay – not the market return. Series 7: CRISIL PP-MLD AA+ / Stable
7Weekly valuation
An AMFI-appointed valuer sets a fair value every week. It is what your MLD is worth today. Published on the issuer’s website
8Buy-back window
Dates when the issuer offers to buy MLDs back before maturity, usually at a small discount. Kaveri: every quarter at the latest weekly value minus 1.5%, from Jan 2027

Who MLDs suit – and who should skip

You’ll answer a short suitability check before you can apply.

Can suit you if

  • ✓You can leave the money untouched for 1.5 to 5 years
  • ✓You can accept 0% in a bad market, for more than an FD if it holds up
  • ✓You stick to issuers with strong ratings and clean MLD records

Skip MLDs if

  • ×You may need the money before maturity
  • ×You can’t afford years with no return

Key risks

“Principal protected” does not mean risk-free. Read these before you apply.
1 The issuer can default If the NBFC can’t pay, you may lose part or all of your money. Protection depends on the issuer.
2 Condition fails → 0% If the index ends below the barrier, you get only ₹1,00,000 back after years with no return.
3 Upside is capped Even if the Nifty doubles, Series 7 pays at most 22.5% in total.
4 Hard to exit early Few trades on BSE. Buy-back windows are quarterly and at a discount to the weekly value.

Frequently asked questions

Is my principal guaranteed?

No. It is protected at maturity only if the issuer pays. That is why every product shows the issuer’s PP-MLD rating, its finances and its past MLD record.

What if the Nifty falls more than 20% in Series 7?

You get ₹1,00,000 back at maturity and earn 0%. You don’t lose principal from the fall, but you lose what an FD would have earned.

Can I get out before maturity?

Two ways: sell on BSE (few trades, so the price can be low), or use the issuer’s quarterly buy-back window at the latest weekly value minus 1.5%.

Why is the final level an average of 3 days?

So a single bad day at the very end doesn’t decide your return. Series 7 averages the Nifty closes on 23, 24 and 25 Oct 2028.

How does Stockify get paid?

The issuer pays Stockify 1.0% of face value, one time (₹1,000 per debenture). You pay no fee to Stockify. We show this on every product.

What do I need to apply?

PAN, KYC and a demat account with NSDL or CDSL, plus a short suitability check. Resident individuals, HUFs, companies and trusts can apply now; NRIs later.
