## Understanding MLDs
**What is an MLD?**
A Market Linked Debenture is a structured product. It pairs a debt component, which protects the principal, with exposure to an underlying such as the Nifty 50 or gold, which drives the return.

**How are returns determined?**
Each MLD sets out its payout before you invest: the underlying, the participation rate and the return range. Your return falls within that range, based on how the underlying performs.
Example: 1.7x participation in Nifty 50 gains, up to a maximum return of 51%.

**How is the principal protected?**
The principal is returned at maturity, subject to the issuer's ability to pay. Some MLDs protect it fully. Others protect it against a set fall in the index, for example up to 20%.

**Liquidity and taxation**
MLDs are listed on BSE and can be sold before maturity, subject to demand. Gains are taxed at your income-tax slab rate.
